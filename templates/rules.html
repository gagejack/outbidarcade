Rules

1. Pay what you want

Minimum {{ min_first | money }}, whole US dollars, no maximum worth mentioning. There is no fixed price for a spot because there are no spots for sale, only numbers.

2. The amount you pay is your score

The board sorts by score, highest first. Ties go to whoever got there first. That is the entire ranking algorithm.

3. Top-ups stack

Add money to a listing any time from your private listing page. Every confirmed bid adds to the same running total, so a game that paid {{ min_first | money }} four times sits at {{ (min_first * 4) | money }}.

4. Bids never expire, and never come back

Your listing stays up and your total stays counted. Nothing is refunded when somebody passes you, which is exactly why the top number only ever goes up. Bid what you would be happy to have spent on a good ad.

5. Games only

Video games, demos, mods, game jams, playable prototypes, storefront pages. Anything you can actually play or wishlist. No crypto, no gambling, no adult content, no malware, nothing illegal. Listings that break this get removed without a refund.

6. Every listing is checked before it goes live

You reserve a spot, you pay, the operator confirms the payment, the listing appears. Nothing on the board is there without a real payment behind it.

7. This is entertainment

It is a dollar auction with cover art. It is not an investment, the ranking means nothing about quality, and the money is spent the moment it is sent.
